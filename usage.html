Financelytic Usage Guide

Dashboard

The Dashboard provides a complete financial overview including monthly income, debt-to-income ratio, overdue bills, upcoming payments, and financial insights.

Budget Planning

Financelytic uses the 50/30/20 budgeting structure to help organize needs, wants, savings, and debt management.

Expense Tracking

Users can create recurring expenses with customizable frequencies including daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly schedules.

Mortgage Tools

Mortgage calculations use standard amortization formulas to estimate payoff timelines and payment progress.

AI Budget Assistant

The AI Budget Assistant uses Apple on-device intelligence to generate budgeting suggestions using your financial data directly on your device.
