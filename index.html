Keep Your Money Organized

Financelytic is an iOS personal finance app designed to help you log income, track expenses, monitor bills, manage loans, review mortgage progress, and stay on top of your day-to-day finances in one place.

Core Features

Financelytic brings together everyday money tracking tools in a clear, privacy-focused interface built for simple logging, planning, and financial visibility.

Dashboard

View your income, debt-to-income ratio, overdue bills, and financial summary in one clean dashboard.

Budget Overview

Review your spending with a simple budgeting breakdown and see how your monthly activity compares across key categories.

Expense Tracking

Log income and expenses, track recurring bills, and review payment statuses with flexible category organization.

Mortgage Payoff

Estimate mortgage payoff dates with built-in amortization calculations and payment logging.

Loan Management

Track personal loans and monthly obligations so you can better understand how debt affects your overall budget.

Privacy First

Your financial data stays on your device, giving you more privacy and control over your personal records.
